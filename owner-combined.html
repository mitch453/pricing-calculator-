Rebate rules and notes

Commercial battery PRCs (BESS3/4/5) commence 1 September 2026 — installs before that date earn none. New solar is not required (PDRS Amendment No. 3, from 7 Sep 2026): BESS4/5 get the higher rate only when the battery goes in within 90 days of new solar ≥ 25% of usable capacity, otherwise the lower rate (0.067 vs 0.1 kW/kWh). BESS3 needs only the 90 days. BESS3/4 also need a CEC-listed battery — SigenStack is listed only as 4–8 module stacks on a BST controller, so bigger stacks earn no BESS3/4 PRCs. Usable capacity = 90% of nominal per the PDRS Rule. Counted capacity is capped at 4h × battery-inverter kW. Certificates trade EX GST (PRC ~$3.05, STC ~$38) — the defaults here are their inc-GST equivalents ($3.36 / $41.80).

Overheads

Weekly overhead and the net target set the recommended price on the Sigenergy and GoodWe tabs (with Installs Per Week on each tab; working days only splits the profit card into days). Every job is priced so the business nets the target % of ex-GST revenue: price = (net cost + extras + commission + 1.1 × weekly overhead ÷ installs/wk) ÷ (1 − target). Daily overhead = weekly ÷ working days (currently / day). Persists in this browser.
