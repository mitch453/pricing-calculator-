Sigen C&I — Commercial Job Configuration (all prices INC GST)

SigenStack commercial systems: 4–21 × BAT 12.0 modules (12.06kWh nominal each) under ONE battery controller, DC-coupled to a Sigen C&I hybrid inverter. PV-only inverters add extra solar on the AC side and cannot connect the battery. Bases, covers, cables and fixation kits are derived automatically. Everything on this tab is inc GST (supplier list prices grossed up 10%); certificates are also shown at inc-GST equivalents.

Rebates — NSW PDRS PRCs + Federal STCs

Commercial battery PRCs (BESS3/4/5) commence 1 September 2026 — installs before that date earn none. BESS4/BESS5 require new solar ≥ 25% of usable battery capacity at the site (hard eligibility rule). Usable capacity = 90% of nominal per the PDRS Rule. Counted capacity is capped at 4h × battery-inverter kW. Certificates trade EX GST (PRC ~$3.05, STC ~$38) — the defaults here are their inc-GST equivalents ($3.36 / $41.80).

Deye C&I — GE-F120 Commercial BESS (all prices INC GST)

Deye GE-F120-2H2: outdoor all-in-one cabinet — 122.8kWh nominal LiFePO4 + integrated 50kW three-phase hybrid PCS, aircon, aerosol fire suppression, off-grid/black start. Up to 65kW of PV input per cabinet (4 MPPTs); up to 10 cabinets in parallel. IP55, 10-year warranty. PDRS usable capacity = 90% × nominal = 110.5kWh per cabinet — over the 100kWh BESS4 threshold, so the FULL 90 PRC/kWh rate applies to every kWh.

Rebates — NSW PDRS PRCs + Solar STCs

Commercial battery PRCs (BESS4/5) commence 1 September 2026. BESS4/5 require new solar ≥ 25% of usable battery capacity (27.6kW per cabinet). At 122.8kWh nominal this unit is never eligible for federal battery STCs (100kWh nominal cap) — PRCs are the whole battery rebate. Certificates trade EX GST (PRC ~$3.05); defaults below are inc-GST equivalents.
