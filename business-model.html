Capacity & Revenue

Revenue is capacity-driven — assumes sales keep pace with install capacity. Commission prefill ≈ $25 × 20 panels + $650 battery = $1,150/job.

Team — Roles & Salaries

Salaries are placeholders — edit to your real numbers. Per crew staffing scales automatically with crew count; Office headcount is independent of crews. On-costs (super, workers comp, leave loading) are applied to all wages.

Vehicles & Fixed Overhead

Fixed overhead is non-wage only: rent, marketing, insurance, fuel, software — everything NOT listed in the team table. Don't re-enter wages here — your ~$125k/week total overhead already includes wages; this field is the remainder. The prefill is a placeholder split — edit it.

Annual Comparison

Assumptions

• Annual = weekly × working weeks, so weekly figures (overhead, vehicles) are only counted for working weeks — bump them up if they run 52 weeks.
• Salaries are annual and are NOT scaled by working weeks.
• Sales are assumed to keep up with install capacity (capacity-driven revenue).
• Gross profit per install is component-only GP (after materials & STCs, before commissions/wages/overhead) — same basis as the weekly reports.
• Net margin % is net profit as a share of gross profit (sale revenue isn't modelled here).
• Everything saves in this browser only.
