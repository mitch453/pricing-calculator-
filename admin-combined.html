Overheads

Weekly overhead and the net target set the recommended price (with Installs Per Week on each tab; working days only splits the profit card into days). Every job is priced so the business nets the target % of ex-GST revenue: price = (net cost + extras + commission + 1.1 × weekly overhead ÷ installs/wk) ÷ (1 − target). Daily overhead = weekly ÷ working days (currently / day). Applies to both tabs and persists in this browser.
